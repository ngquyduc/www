About

I like the parts of software where the numbers matter.

I'm Duc, a mobile software engineer at TikTok in Singapore. I work on the instant-messaging SDK that TikTok's chat is built on, and that TikTok Shop and Lemon8 also use. I started on iOS, and the team is now doing more and more cross-platform work.

Before TikTok I was an iOS developer intern at Kovalee, shipping about two apps a month and maintaining their analytics and in-app-purchase SDKs. Before that I wrote C++ services and a SolidJS UI for a point-cloud surveillance system at dConstruct Robotics, and worked on MongoDB performance at Vebits. I studied Computer Science at NUS.
