Home hero: “What users see” vs “What I see”, message types and states

MOBILE RULES

  • Under 720 px of container width the panes become two tabs (role="tablist"): What users see (default) and What I see. State is shared: send a photo on one tab and watch its upload on the other.
  • The phone fills the width (max 420 px). The thread scrolls (newest at the bottom); the + tray wraps.
  • Long press works with touch (450 ms, native callout suppressed). A short tap shows a hint pill instead.
