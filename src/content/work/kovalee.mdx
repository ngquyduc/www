## Context

I joined Kovalee in Paris for five months during the NUS Overseas Colleges programme. The goal was speed: get new iOS apps in education and health in front of users quickly, and learn from their feedback.

## What I built

**iOS apps in SwiftUI**, about two a month. Reusable SwiftUI kits and Fastlane release automation cut time to launch by about 20 hours.

**The shared SDKs for analytics, in-app purchases and ads**, used by about 20 apps. I optimized and maintained them, which improved app performance and raised monetization efficiency by 15%.

## Decisions

<Callout kind="Decision" title="Shared SDKs for what every app needs, kits for what each app shows">
  Analytics, in-app purchases and ads went into shared SDKs: every app needs them, they touch revenue, and a fix should reach all of the apps at once. Screens went into reusable SwiftUI kits instead, which each app could copy and change freely. The cost is a careful release process for the SDKs, because one regression reaches about 20 apps.
</Callout>

## In hindsight

I'd have put automated tests around the in-app purchase SDK earlier. At two new apps a month, the slowest step was checking by hand that purchases still worked in every app after an SDK change, and tests would have turned that into a few minutes in CI.
